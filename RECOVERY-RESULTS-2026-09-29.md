## Recovery verdict and next gate

The no-relevant-speech route now works **operationally through a bounded interval**: original audio → audio-first model observation → comparison with Whisper/Qwen/VAD/source → backed-up Turkish repair or reviewed gap → English no-cue decision → sampled export rendering. In these two development intervals, it treated apparent recognition text over music as unsupported without deciding by model vote; independent accuracy has not been established. The earlier E039 short-reply case operationally retained real dialogue, and its long-hold repair restored omitted turns; the E038 contextual passage retained a supported correct candidate. Their evidence and limitations remain in the earlier checkpoint.

The route has **not** shown that it can settle conflicting exact Turkish words. Historical E039 cue 749 (`çağıracağız` / `uçuracağız`) and cue 757 (`duygu sömürüsü` / `duygusuzluk`) still have conflicting saved Gemini, Scribe, and local evidence. Those are legitimate unresolved cases, not proof that the audio is inherently unrecoverable. E021's post-32 s onset and E007's adjoining credit intervals lack approved audio coverage. The current blocker is **insufficient evidence for those intervals**, plus a **model-disagreement limit** for E039 exact wording. It is not a missing `no_relevant_speech` value or a demand for unanimous recognizer agreement. An independently adjudicated Turkish listen to the smallest disputed turns, or a separately authorized stronger targeted audio review, is the next useful test. No additional clip or call is authorized by this experiment.

The workflow can save an unresolved source decision, defer its English dependency, retain settled translations elsewhere, and rebase after a repair. That continuation was demonstrated on real E039 cues and a private source-edit probe; **full-episode autonomous completion remains untested**. The unresolved scenes may remain deferred while unrelated source, English, alignment, and render work proceeds. They still block a final complete release.

**Decision: ready to attempt development integration after a bounded spoken-dialogue test; not qualified for production use.** Ordinary disputed words remain a known limitation to measure, not an entry condition requiring every historical E039 case to be solved. The next test uses representative spoken intervals in existing development material, then one complete development-episode integration run under the reusable prompt. That run must account for every scene even if the episode proves unreleasable. No new provider use is authorized by the four-call experiment. The workflow will be considered for a fresh held-out run only after the integration result is assessed; no new held-out cohort starts automatically.
