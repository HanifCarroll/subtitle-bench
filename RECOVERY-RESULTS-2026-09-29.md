## Recovery verdict and next gate

The no-relevant-speech route now works **through a bounded interval**: original audio → independent audio-first observation → comparison with Whisper/Qwen/VAD/source → backed-up Turkish repair or reviewed gap → English no-cue decision → sampled export rendering. It can distinguish a recognition hallucination over music from a missing subtitle in these two development intervals without deciding by model vote. The earlier E039 short-reply case operationally retained real dialogue, and its long-hold repair restored omitted turns; the E038 contextual passage retained a supported correct candidate. Their evidence and limitations remain in the earlier checkpoint.

The route has **not** shown that it can settle conflicting exact Turkish words. Historical E039 cue 749 (`çağıracağız` / `uçuracağız`) and cue 757 (`duygu sömürüsü` / `duygusuzluk`) still have conflicting saved Gemini, Scribe, and local evidence. Those are legitimate unresolved cases, not proof that the audio is inherently unrecoverable. E021's post-32 s onset and E007's adjoining credit intervals lack approved audio coverage. The current blocker is **insufficient evidence for those intervals**, plus a **model-disagreement limit** for E039 exact wording. It is not a missing `no_relevant_speech` value or a demand for unanimous recognizer agreement. An independently adjudicated Turkish listen to the smallest disputed turns, or a separately authorized stronger targeted audio review, is the next useful test. No additional clip or call is authorized by this experiment.

The workflow can save an unresolved source decision, defer its English dependency, retain settled translations elsewhere, and rebase after a repair. That continuation was demonstrated on real E039 cues and a private source-edit probe; **full-episode autonomous completion remains untested**. The unresolved scenes may remain deferred while unrelated source, English, alignment, and render work proceeds. They still block a final complete release.

**Decision: not ready for the one full development-episode integration run yet.** This experiment established the acoustic no-speech branch, but did not establish a recovery or acceptable final fallback for ordinary disputed spoken words. Starting a long episode now would repeat the known E039 exact-word and evidence-permission bottleneck. The appropriate next milestone is a bounded exact-word/onset recovery test on existing development material, with new authorization before any additional provider audio use. After that route works, run one full development episode under the reusable prompt, finish a candidate pair and dry release check, and only then freeze and try one fresh held-out episode. The existing approval covers no such additional calls or episodes.
