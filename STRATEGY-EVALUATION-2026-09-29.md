All 72 scene assets match their recorded hashes, all 48 SRTs parse with cues inside clip bounds, all 24 media durations match the interval within 81 ms, and the 75-file ZIP integrity check passed. This validates the package, not subtitle content.

A competent independent Turkish/English reviewer should first compare both versions against the audio/video without seeing the key or model reasons, marking omitted intelligible speech, unsupported words, material English changes, and important timing defects with clip times. Natural equivalent translations count as acceptable. One reviewer of all 12 scenes is the smallest useful independent assessment; a second adjudicator is useful only for disputed material items. Neutral follow-up questions can be used after first-pass observations are saved. No such reviewer or trusted reference was available locally, so there is **no independent E040 accuracy, false-alarm, missed-error, or incorrect-repair rate** to report. No media has been sent outside the machine.

**Next decision:** Obtain the independent blind assessment of this already fixed local package, with separate authorization before any outside transfer, hire, or charge. If current changed cues are better and unflagged dialogue is sound, keep the strategy and bound the remaining execution. If repairs damage originally correct lines or most flagged scenes are false alarms, improve review and adjudication. If failures concentrate in truly ambiguous audio after competent listening, expand targeted evidence collection. The sample will identify a development direction; it will not qualify the workflow on unseen episodes by itself.
