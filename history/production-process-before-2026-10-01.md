# Subtitle production process

The product is a reusable agent process that can finish a new Turkish episode with timed Turkish and English subtitles from its original video and audio. An episode is a development or qualification case. Existing subtitles are comparison references opened only after the independent pair is frozen. Keep model opinions, release checks, fallible references, and independently established answers distinct.

## Operating route

The producing agent receives a video, private output directory, and any current provider authorization. Keep existing subtitle candidates, sidecars of unknown provenance, and reference-derived episode notes out of its context. Save media identity, transcripts, decisions, clips, and receipts outside Git. Use `scripts/subtitle-workbench.py` for the case and `docs/guides/WORKBENCH.md` for command syntax. Never change installed sidecars during qualification; finish with a candidate pair and a dry release report. Installation and native playback need their separate authorization and verification.

1. **Inventory the video and provenance.** Record its hash, duration, audio properties, and selected tool/model revisions. Reuse episode-specific recognition only if its receipts establish that it came from this original video independently of any reference. Do not expose existing subtitle files or unknown sidecars to the producing agent.
2. **Transcribe from the video.** Use local Whisper large-v3, 30-second cores with two-second overlap: `python3 scripts/subtitle-workbench.py transcribe VIDEO CASE/transcription --language tr --model /Users/hanifcarroll/.local/share/transcribe-audio/models/ggml-large-v3.bin --chunk-seconds 30 --overlap-seconds 2`. Then run `python3 scripts/join-clip-drafts.py CASE/transcription/manifest.json CASE/core-draft --core-only` to select one provisional cue per overlap core. This replaces per-episode cue-joining code. It does not filter hallucinated words or resolve the saved seams. Keep all raw clips, receipts, and both seam reports for review. Reuse a matching run; a changed model, video, clip, or incomplete output requires recovery.
3. **Decide Turkish source wording.** Open an `audit` case on the core draft. If English exists, run `episode-check`; otherwise run the full `audio-check` now and `episode-check` after the agent-authored English draft exists. Group overlapping source, ASR, coverage, and layout questions by scene. Inspect original-audio clips and timestamped frames when scene context matters. Use focused Whisper large-v3 on shorter clips and the independent Qwen full-pass transcript; retry music or overlap with a separated stem only as supplementary evidence. A disagreement triggers investigation, including accepted lines whose object, speaker, action, or polarity conflicts with the scene. Record one of four outcomes: supported wording (`reviewed` or `resolved`), still awaiting investigation (`unresolved` with a specific missing action), genuinely unintelligible speech (`unresolved` plus `audible_outcome: unintelligible_speech` after audio-capable evidence), or no relevant speech (`reviewed` or `resolved` plus a supported non-speech audible outcome). Do not insert `[Konuşma anlaşılmıyor]` for unfinished investigation. Before English exists, record source-only repairs; once both tracks exist, use paired repairs. Recheck each scene and resume after a deferred one. A recognizer's agreement or forced alignment does not prove the words.

   For an interval resolved by two current local ASR runs and the agent's explicit comparison, `scripts/local-audio-review.py` builds a hash-bound `local_asr_agent` receipt from the completed Whisper run, the current Qwen audio report, and a small agent-authored decision. The decision states whether the agent actually heard the original audio; the normal value is `false`. Use this route only when spoken words are supported by the evidence and context. A material model conflict, possible quiet reply, or suspected absence of relevant speech needs further original-audio evidence or an authorized audio-capable review. The receipt makes a decision traceable; it is not an independent accuracy reference.
4. **Produce and check English.** Translate settled Turkish directly as the agent. `scripts/agent-translate.py` checkpoints batches; its `import` command accepts any sized agent-authored, source-hash-bound JSON set when batch-by-batch commands are cumbersome. Both routes accept an explicit `defer_reason` in place of English for a disputed cue. A `--partial` export contains only settled cues and lists excluded source IDs; it is not a release candidate. A source edit retains translations for unambiguous unchanged cues while invalidating the changed neighborhood. Resolve deferred cues and export a complete draft before final release. Review accepted content as well as deferrals: check negation, who acts or speaks, instructions, and concrete objects against current Turkish and scene context. If Turkish is wrong, repair it first and retranslate; if Turkish is supported but English changes meaning, repair English alone. Preserve correct controls and natural equivalent wording. Align corrected Turkish words, inspect timing, retime both tracks where needed, and render sampled frames. Local text models do not review or translate for the agent.
5. **Freeze an independent outcome.** Run the release dry check and inspect actual candidate files. Record `production_complete`, `partial`, or `failure`, all unresolved speech and meaning questions, runtime, provider usage, and human interventions. Freeze copies of both SRTs, their hashes, workflow/configuration, and the outcome before opening episode references. A passed structural gate alone is not an accuracy result. If a qualified candidate is later installed, verify IINA playback separately.
6. **Compare after the freeze.** Validate the Turkish and English reference files against the same episode and video cut. Align copies, including local cut differences; never rewrite the frozen pair. Compare corresponding dialogue, timing, and segmentation against original media and context. Accept equivalent English wording, treat the references as fallible, and record any later reference-assisted correction separately as development work.

## Failure resolution routes

| Failure | Detect | Gather evidence | Resolve | Verify | Stop when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wrong episode, cut, offset, or drift in a comparison reference | File identity, multiple distinctive anchors across the cut, sampled audio | Frozen independent pair, reference copy, opening/middle/tail clips, offset report | Reject the reference for that span or align a comparison-only copy | Recheck anchors and audible starts/ends across the cut | Comparison spans are aligned or excluded explicitly |
| ASR repetition, empty result, or truncation | Repeated tokens, empty clip receipt, seam report, full-window status | Raw clips and logs, shorter overlapping original-audio windows, independent recognizer | Retranscribe targeted intervals and replace the loop; do not copy repeated text | Confirm dialogue before, within, and after interval; full scan remains contiguous | No loop hides an intelligible turn |
| Omitted short reply or dialogue under a long cue | Source gaps, bidirectional ASR comparison, long holds, scene review | Original clip with context, focused recognition, visual speaker context | Insert/split paired cues for supported speech | Replay interval on video clock; compare each turn and render both tracks | Every intelligible turn has a timed source and English cue |
| Unsupported addition or wrong Turkish word | Subtitle-to-audio disagreement, odd context, independent source discrepancy | Original audio and independent recognition views where useful | Remove or correct only when evidence supports the change; preserve correct text on model disagreement | Recheck the entire affected sentence and neighboring turns | No unsupported speech remains, or a genuine lexical uncertainty is documented |
| Overlap, music, or disputed audio | Conflicting models, uncertain words, recognition over music | Original mix, short context clips, optional separation, independent audio review if authorized | Use recoverable words; avoid inventing lyrics or speakers. Escalate only the unresolved interval | Check original mix again against corrected cue and timing | Intelligible content is recovered; a truly unrecoverable span is explicitly counted |
| Idiom, referent, polarity, or contextual English error | Direct source-linked semantic review and scene context | Corrected Turkish, adjacent turns, timestamped frames when relevant, established lexical source if needed | Agent writes a meaning-preserving English cue; retain acceptable alternative phrasing | Re-read the delivered English against current Turkish and context | No material meaning error or unsupported English addition remains |
| Early, late, held, dense, or badly segmented cue | Aligned-word diagnostics, long-cue and cross-turn flags, visual render | Original audio, accepted words, neighboring cue boundaries, frame samples | Retime/split paired tracks without omitting speech; allow different English segmentation | Re-align affected words, rerun layout and render checks | No cue visibly crosses a new turn or material timing defect remains |
| Chunk seam loses or duplicates words | Seam report, overlapping raw clips, duplicate/missing phrases | Both clip outputs and original seam audio | Reconstruct the phrase from overlap and correct the joined draft | Check context on both sides and rerun full source comparison | Boundary text matches supported speech once |
| Provider failure, stale receipt, or cost limit | Call receipt, hash/config mismatch, incomplete output, usage ledger | Exact clip/model/prompt hashes and provider response | Retry only within active authorization; otherwise preserve a partial result | Match saved result to input and account for usage and deletion | Valid evidence exists, or the case is explicitly partial and permission-limited |

**Unrecoverable audio** is a span with confirmed speech whose lexical content remains indeterminate after checking the original mix, overlapping context, and targeted independent recognition or an available trustworthy source. Masking, clipping, or simultaneous speech may cause this. A plausible recognizer guess is not enough. Use a timed `[Anlaşılmayan konuşma]` / `[Indistinct speech]` cue only for such a span; record its duration and count it as unresolved content in qualification. Songs may use `[Şarkı söylüyor]` / `[Singing]` only where words are genuinely unrecoverable or not spoken lexically. Ordinary intelligible dialogue must be resolved.

## Capability limits and circular dependencies

- Hashes, paired edits, complete scan receipts, rollback, and rendering prevent stale or inconsistent output. They do not recover words, decide English meaning, or prove timing against audio.
- WhisperX aligns supplied Turkish. A wrong supplied word can still receive a plausible timestamp. The aligned source cannot certify itself.
- The agent can review English accurately only after Turkish is corrected. English cannot independently certify the source. A semantic gate over a wrong source can pass a faithful mistranslation.
- Qwen, Whisper, Gemini, and Scribe may disagree or share errors. Agreement is evidence, not an independent reference. Repeated local questions without a resolution route can consume the entire episode budget.
- Full Episode 38–40 scans and repairs found real omissions and timing defects. The fresh-context Episode 18 independent run produced a partial pair with ordinary unresolved dialogue and no release; it did not demonstrate autonomous completion or independently measured accuracy.

This table records the earlier strategy decision; its reference-first branch is outside the current independent-production objective. The current route begins with the original video and large-v3, uses independent audio evidence and agent source decisions, freezes the bilingual pair, then compares it with any existing subtitles. Episode 18 exposed incomplete source decisions despite full local transcription and selected authorized audio review. Neither that run nor the earlier clips establish final accuracy or autonomous completion.
