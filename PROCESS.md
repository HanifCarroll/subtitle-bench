# Subtitle production process

The product is a reusable agent process that can finish a new Turkish episode with timed Turkish and English subtitles. An episode is a development or qualification case. The original audio controls Turkish wording; a cut-matched subtitle is a useful candidate, not ground truth. Keep model opinions, release checks, and independently established answers distinct.

## Operating route

Inputs are a video and, optionally, Turkish and English SRT candidates. Save media, candidates, transcripts, decisions, clips, and receipts outside Git. Use `scripts/subtitle-workbench.py` for the case and `WORKBENCH.md` for command syntax. Never change installed sidecars during qualification; finish with a candidate pair and a dry release report. For a normal production run, installation and native playback need their separate authorization and verification.

1. **Inventory and choose a source candidate.** Validate file identity, language, encoding, duration, and cut. If a Turkish reference exists, make an independent video-clock ASR draft and run `scripts/reference-source.py VIDEO ASR.tr.srt REFERENCE.tr.srt NEW-DIRECTORY`. Its shifted SRT is only a timing proposal. Select it only after original-audio checks at opening, middle, tail, and suspicious intervals show that it preserves dialogue and timing. Otherwise start from ASR. An English SRT can reveal missing turns but cannot establish Turkish words.
2. **For video-only input, transcribe without a source prerequisite.** Use local Whisper large-v3, 30-second cores with two-second overlap: `python3 scripts/subtitle-workbench.py transcribe VIDEO CASE/transcription --language tr --model /Users/hanifcarroll/.local/share/transcribe-audio/models/ggml-large-v3.bin --chunk-seconds 30 --overlap-seconds 2`. The raw clip SRTs, seam report, completion receipts, and joined draft remain inspectable. Empty clips, seams, and repeated phrases are questions. Reuse a matching run; a changed model, video, clip, or incomplete output requires recovery, not blind reuse.
3. **Audit and repair Turkish.** Open an `audit` case, run `episode-check`, and group overlapping source, ASR, coverage, and layout questions by scene. Inspect original-audio clips and timestamped frames when scene context matters. Use focused Whisper large-v3 on shorter clips and the independent Qwen full-pass transcript; retry music or overlap with a separated stem only as supplementary evidence. Apply supported bilingual paired repairs. Recheck after each scene. A recognizer's agreement or forced alignment does not prove the words.
4. **Produce and check English.** Translate the corrected Turkish directly as the agent. Existing English is a candidate. Link every source utterance, review context and meaning directly, and repair only supported errors. Accept equivalent English wording. Align corrected Turkish words, inspect the timing report, retime both tracks where needed, and render sampled frames. Local text models do not review or translate for the agent.
5. **Stop with an outcome.** Run the release dry check and inspect actual candidate files. Record `production_complete`, `partial`, or `failure`, all unresolved speech and meaning questions, runtime, provider usage, and human interventions. A passed structural gate alone is not an accuracy result. If a qualified candidate is later installed, verify IINA playback separately.

## Failure resolution routes

| Failure | Detect | Gather evidence | Resolve | Verify | Stop when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wrong episode, cut, offset, or drift | File identity, multiple distinctive anchors across the cut, sampled audio | Original candidate, independent ASR draft, opening/middle/tail clips, offset report | Reject the reference or create a shifted candidate; repair local drift only with audio support | Recheck anchors and audible starts/ends; rerun source audit | Source candidate fits the entire cut, or ASR becomes the base |
| ASR repetition, empty result, or truncation | Repeated tokens, empty clip receipt, seam report, full-window status | Raw clips and logs, shorter overlapping original-audio windows, independent recognizer | Retranscribe targeted intervals and replace the loop; do not copy repeated text | Confirm dialogue before, within, and after interval; full scan remains contiguous | No loop hides an intelligible turn |
| Omitted short reply or dialogue under a long cue | Source gaps, bidirectional ASR comparison, reference-only lines, long holds, scene review | Original clip with context, focused recognition, candidate reference, visual speaker context | Insert/split paired cues for supported speech | Replay interval on video clock; compare each turn and render both tracks | Every intelligible turn has a timed source and English cue |
| Unsupported addition or wrong Turkish word | Subtitle-to-audio disagreement, odd context, independent source discrepancy | Original audio, two independent ASR views where useful, cut-matched reference | Remove or correct only when evidence supports the change; preserve correct text on model disagreement | Recheck the entire affected sentence and neighboring turns | No unsupported speech remains, or a genuine lexical uncertainty is documented |
| Overlap, music, or disputed audio | Conflicting models, uncertain words, recognition over music | Original mix, short context clips, optional separation, independent audio review if authorized | Use recoverable words; avoid inventing lyrics or speakers. Escalate only the unresolved interval | Check original mix again against corrected cue and timing | Intelligible content is recovered; a truly unrecoverable span is explicitly counted |
| Idiom, referent, polarity, or contextual English error | Direct source-linked semantic review and scene context | Corrected Turkish, adjacent turns, timestamped frames when relevant, established lexical source if needed | Agent writes a meaning-preserving English cue; retain acceptable alternative phrasing | Re-read the delivered English against current Turkish and context | No material meaning error or unsupported English addition remains |
| Early, late, held, dense, or badly segmented cue | Aligned-word diagnostics, long-cue and cross-turn flags, visual render | Original audio, accepted words, neighboring cue boundaries, frame samples | Retime/split paired tracks without omitting speech; allow different English segmentation | Re-align affected words, rerun layout and render checks | No cue visibly crosses a new turn or material timing defect remains |
| Chunk seam loses or duplicates words | Seam report, overlapping raw clips, duplicate/missing phrases | Both clip outputs and original seam audio | Reconstruct the phrase from overlap and correct the joined draft | Check context on both sides and rerun full source comparison | Boundary text matches supported speech once |
| Provider failure, stale receipt, or cost limit | Call receipt, hash/config mismatch, incomplete output, usage ledger | Exact clip/model/prompt hashes and provider response | Retry only within active authorization; otherwise preserve a partial result | Match saved result to input and account for usage and deletion | Valid evidence exists, or the case is explicitly partial and permission-limited |

**Unrecoverable audio** is a span with confirmed speech whose lexical content remains indeterminate after checking the original mix, overlapping context, and targeted independent recognition or an available trustworthy source. Masking, clipping, or simultaneous speech may cause this. A plausible recognizer guess is not enough. Use a timed `[Anlaşılmayan konuşma]` / `[Indistinct speech]` cue only for such a span; record its duration and count it as unresolved content in qualification. Songs may use `[Şarkı söylüyor]` / `[Singing]` only where words are genuinely unrecoverable or not spoken lexically. Ordinary intelligible dialogue must be resolved.

## Capability limits and circular dependencies

- Hashes, paired edits, complete scan receipts, rollback, and rendering prevent stale or inconsistent output. They do not recover words, decide English meaning, or prove timing against audio.
- WhisperX aligns supplied Turkish. A wrong supplied word can still receive a plausible timestamp. The aligned source cannot certify itself.
- The agent can review English accurately only after Turkish is corrected. English cannot independently certify the source. A semantic gate over a wrong source can pass a faithful mistranslation.
- Qwen, Whisper, Gemini, and Scribe may disagree or share errors. Agreement is evidence, not an independent reference. Repeated local questions without a resolution route can consume the entire episode budget.
- Full Episode 38–40 scans and repairs have found real omissions and timing defects, but no fresh-agent run has yet demonstrated full autonomous completion. The documented route is a candidate process until held-out results and independent adjudication establish otherwise.

## Development comparison that selected the route

The comparable local test used the same 68 seconds of original-audio clips: Episode 39's 43-second former long hold, its 8-second short exchange, and Episode 38's 17-second contextual `şekerim` scene. Outputs and timings are in the private `Workflow/pilots/process-qualification-2026-09-29/development-asr/` directory. Existing Gemini and Scribe trials on those exact clips are in `EXPERIMENTS-2026-09-28.md`.

| Route | Observed result on these cases | Runtime and service cost | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing turbo draft plus repair | Turbo repeated a vocalization across most of the 43-second scene, losing intervening dialogue; it changed `Bir şeyim yok` to `Bir şey mi yok` in the short exchange. Episode 38, 39, and 40 scans found further issues. | 13.85 seconds total local ASR for the three clips; no service charge. Downstream repair time is not measured. | Development cases have scans and candidate repairs, not a fresh autonomous completed episode. |
| Stronger initial Whisper large-v3 plus independent check | Recovered most dialogue following the vocalization and the short reply. It still made errors in Episode 38 (`Birer kahve içelim` became `Ver kahve içeyim`) and some music/overlap words. | 22.77 seconds total local ASR, about 1.64× turbo; no service charge. Full-episode runtime and repair cost are unmeasured. | Better first-pass evidence on these clips, not an established final-quality improvement. |
| Cut-matched Turkish source first | The Episode 38 reference has 224 agreeing distinctive anchors across the cut after a 4.89-second offset and preserves the known coffee and sugar lines better than either local ASR output. It still requires audio checks; Episode 39 had no supplied suitable reference in this comparison. | Reference proposal took under one second after inputs existed; acquisition time, downstream review, and any provider cost are unmeasured. | Promising when a suitable file exists; cannot be the required video-only path. |

The selected route is **audio-checked reference first when suitable, otherwise large-v3 first**, with a full independent local audio scan and targeted repair. It is a provisional engineering choice. The comparative evidence does not measure final episode quality, incorrect repairs, or autonomous completion; those are qualification outcomes, not implied by this table.
