| Is a stronger first pass worth its extra local runtime? | Same 68 seconds: E39's 43-second held-vocalization scene, its eight-second reply, and E38's 17-second contextual `şekerim` scene. | Should speech detection or longer chunks replace 30-second cores? | On E016's first 180 seconds, Silero VAD v6.2.0 made the first four 30-second music/credit clips empty instead of repeating likely hallucinated credits. Consider VAD only as supplemental evidence on suspected music hallucinations, with a short-reply countercheck. Longer chunks were not adopted. Artifacts are in `vad-opening/`. |
| Can shorter Qwen windows close repeated or empty output automatically? | In E064 development, a repeated 30-second window at 870–900 seconds remained questionable as a focused 15-second window at 877.5–892.5 seconds. The repeated/empty windows still need a stronger source or authorized audio review. Reports and caches are in `qwen-recovery/`. |
| Can a local decision be traceable without fabricating audio hearing? | On E064's ordinary 210–225-second development interval, the version 2 receipt builder accepted authentic completed Whisper clips, a current pinned Qwen report, exact video/source hashes, and an agent assessment disclosing `heard_original_audio: false`. It does not independently establish words or justify clearing a material conflict. `qwen-recovery/ordinary-210000-receipt.json` and `new-env-smoke-210000.json` hold the artifacts. |
| Does an opening ASR conflict in a video-only case resolve with local retries? | After E007's frozen first run stopped, a **development** check of its 28–62-second original-audio clip found zero Silero v6.2.0 speech segments. VAD and repeated short-window outputs cannot establish either spoken words or confirmed silence. This case becomes a regression for any revised recovery route. Evidence is in `e007-recovery/` and the preserved v2 E007 first-run directory. |
| Can the frozen bundle command recover an E021 disputed interval? | E021's first-run agent supplied the full transcription manifest to an optional flag that expects a focused-window manifest; the command failed. Fixing it can remove an avoidable failure, but it cannot resolve the words. Preserve the 0–30-second conflict as a regression. Evidence is in the v2 E021 `evidence/` directory. |
