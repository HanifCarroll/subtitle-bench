For 064, the supplied Turkish candidate repeated two short phrases in 2,947 of 3,481 cues from 00:23:21.330 onward; the supplied English was also corrupt over that 4,258.150-second span. The reference detector found only 111 agreeing anchors concentrated early and rejected it. The agent initially passed that rejected file into its ASR audit, making 1,612 reference-disagreement flags. In a **development continuation**, removing that rejected reference reduced the queue from 2,239 to 593, but the episode still stopped partial with 36 audio questions and unfinished English. This is a concrete workflow defect, not an improvement in subtitle quality.

For 016, the first run stopped before the eight-hour budget and treated absent English as a stopping point. In a **development continuation**, the same agent authored 1,677 English cues and ran a dry check, showing that English absence was not intrinsically a permission barrier. The current dry check still fails on 306 source questions, 33 audio questions, 58 English timing flags, missing semantic decisions, layout, and render. Turkish WhisperX alignment then failed because the frozen v1 runtime lacked an importable `whisperx` environment. No independent accuracy judgment was made on that pair. An opening sample contains repeated `Altyazı M.K.` and `İzlediğiniz için teşekkür ederim` from large-v3 over the first 118 seconds; silent VAD windows, empty Qwen windows, and the library sidecar's later start make these suspected music/credit hallucinations, not established dialogue. They remain a development regression, not a trusted word-level reference.

For 099, the supplied Turkish candidate had 455 agreeing anchors among 461 matched cues across five cut regions at a 10 ms estimated offset. This supports timing, not exact wording. The agent's source audit still raised hundreds of disagreements with ASR. After 11 decisions, its current dry check failed with the counts above. The coordinator interrupted at 14:00 UTC after 38 minutes without a visible receipt or status reply, but the agent had written that dry check at 13:59:59 UTC; the coordinator's first ledger entry missed it and was explicitly corrected. This run is therefore **inconclusive for what the agent would have finished within eight hours** and is counted as an attempted, non-complete case. It is not evidence that the audio was unrecoverable. Any later continuation must be labeled development.

These runs demonstrate whole-media transcription and full-window comparison, and they demonstrate that rejecting a corrupted candidate can prevent a bad source selection. They do **not** demonstrate reliable Turkish recovery, correct English, well-timed final cues, or autonomous completion. The agent's own semantic decisions and release gate cannot supply an independent error rate. The five preselected evaluation windows per case are in `evaluation-windows-v1.json`; they were selected before results but were not included in the v1 freeze hash (v2 corrects this). No bilingual annotation has been obtained, so omitted-dialogue, material meaning, timing, and incorrect-repair rates are **unmeasured**. Local compute, energy, and developer/agent labor costs are not monetized; provider calls and uploads were zero, with no provider charge incurred in these runs.

Evidence: each case's `runs/NNN-clean/run.json`, `events.jsonl`, transcription receipts, audio report, and release report where present. The freeze and media hashes in those files bind the results to exact inputs and workflow code.
